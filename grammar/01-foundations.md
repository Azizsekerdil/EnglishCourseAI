# English foundations

## Word order

The usual affirmative pattern is **subject + verb + object**: `Mina reads books.` Questions often move an auxiliary before the subject: `Does Mina read books?`

## Articles and countability

Use `a/an` with a singular, non-specific count noun; use `the` when the referent is identifiable. General plurals and uncount nouns often take no article: `Books help us.` `Water is essential.`

## Nouns and pronouns

Learn irregular plurals as complete pairs (`child/children`, `man/men`). Distinguish subject forms (`I, he, they`) from object forms (`me, him, them`).
