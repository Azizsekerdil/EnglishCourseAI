# Verbs and tenses

- Present simple: routines and facts — `She works here.`
- Present continuous: actions in progress — `She is working now.`
- Past simple: completed past events — `She worked yesterday.`
- Past continuous: a past action in progress — `She was working at nine.`
- Present perfect: a past event connected to now — `She has finished.`
- Future: `will` for spontaneous decisions/predictions; `be going to` for plans or visible evidence.

Record irregular verbs as three forms: `go — went — gone`.
