# Sound and writing

English spelling and sound do not map one-to-one. Practise minimal pairs such as `ship/sheep` and `bad/bed`; learn both `th` sounds in `think` and `this`.

The endings `-ed` and `-s` change pronunciation according to the preceding sound: `worked /t/`, `played /d/`, `wanted /ɪd/`; `cats /s/`, `dogs /z/`, `buses /ɪz/`.

Apostrophes and hyphens are meaningful parts of spelling: `don't` differs from `dont`, and `well-known` differs from `well known` in some contexts.
